Why "Grained"

A name with three layers — like the tree it draws.

Our logo tells the whole story: an open Bible, a seed planted in it, growth rings forming around it, and a sprout breaking through. Here's what the name means.

01 — INGRAINED

Faith that becomes part of them

When something is ingrained, it's so deep it's part of who you are. That's Deuteronomy 6 — faith impressed on children at the table, on the road, at bedtime. Not memorised for a quiz. Ingrained for a lifetime.

02 — THE GRAIN OF WHEAT

The seed story of the gospel

"Unless a grain of wheat falls into the ground and dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit." — John 12:24. Every lesson is a seed. We help churches plant them well.

03 — THE GRAIN OF THE TREE

Growth you can see

Wood grain is the record of a tree's growth — every season written into the timber. Grained makes each child's spiritual journey visible: to teachers, to parents, and to the child themselves.
